## Learning Priority Matrix

### HIGH PRIORITY (Start Immediately)
Start with 1-2 tools, not all at once. Build portfolio pieces before adding more.

**Option A: Web-First Approach**
- **Why:** Landing pages have consistent demand, high price point, faster learning curve
- **Timeline:** 4-6 weeks to basic competency
- **Tools to Learn:**
  - Figma (UI/UX design) - Free tier available
  - WordPress or Webflow (website builder)
  - HTML/CSS basics (optional but valuable)

**Option B: Design-First Approach**
- **Why:** Graphic design has lower barrier to entry, rapid portfolio building
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks to basic competency
- **Tools to Learn:**
  - Figma or Canva Pro (easier learning curve)
  - Adobe Canva or Photoshop (more professional)

**Option C: Video-First Approach**
- **Why:** High demand, creative expression, helps with YouTube growth
- **Timeline:** 6-8 weeks to basic competency
- **Tools to Learn:**
  - CapCut (beginner-friendly, free)
  - Adobe Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve (professional)
  - Shotcut (free alternative)

## Recommended Tool Stack

### Web Design & Development
**Primary:** Figma + Webflow
- **Figma:** UI/UX mockups, prototyping, collaboration
- **Webflow:** Website builder with design flexibility
- **Alternative:** WordPress + Elementor (more affordable, steeper learning curve)

**Learning Resources:**
- Figma YouTube tutorials (official channel)
- Webflow University (free courses)
- Udemy: "Complete Web Design Bootcamp"

---

### Video Production & Editing
**Primary:** DaVinci Resolve (Free) or CapCut
- **DaVinci Resolve:** Professional-grade, free version powerful
- **CapCut:** Beginner-friendly, mobile & desktop
- **Alternative:** Adobe Premiere Pro (industry standard but subscription cost)

**Learning Resources:**
- DaVinci Resolve YouTube tutorials
- Casey Faris (YouTube - motion graphics)
- GOAT Filmmaker channel (YouTube)

---

### Graphic Design
**Primary:** Figma + Canva Pro
- **Figma:** Professional vector design, UI/UX focus
- **Canva Pro:** Quick graphics, templates, user-friendly
- **Alternative:** Adobe Creative Suite (expensive but comprehensive)

**Learning Resources:**
- Figma design tutorials (YouTube)
- Canva Design School (official)
- Skillshare: Graphic Design fundamentals

## Suggested Learning Path (12 Weeks)

### WEEKS 1-4: Foundation & First Tool Mastery
**Primary Focus:** Choose ONE service area to master first

**Week 1-2:**
- [ ] Decide which service area to start with
- [ ] Download/install required tools
- [ ] Complete basic tutorials (5-10 hours)
- [ ] Join relevant online communities (Reddit, Discord)

**Week 3-4:**
- [ ] Complete intermediate tutorials

- [ ] Document your learning journey

---

- [ ] Get feedback from online communities

---

## Risk Mitigation

**Problem:** Getting overwhelmed with too many skills

**Solution:** 
- Focus on ONE service for first 6 weeks
- Complete full learning path before taking paid work
- Use portfolio projects to build confidence

---

**Problem:** Lack of client feedback

**Solution:**
- Share work in communities for critique
- Ask friends/family for honest feedback
- Join freelancer mentorship groups
- Analyze competitor portfolios

---

**Problem:** Time management (current job + learning)

**Solution:**
- Commit to 10-15 hours weekly minimum
- Use weekends for focused learning
- Break learning into 30-minute sessions
- Track time with apps like Toggl

## Success Indicators

You're ready to take paid projects when:
- [ ] You can complete 1-2 portfolio projects weekly
