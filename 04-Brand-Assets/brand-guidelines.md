## Typography

### Font Selections (To Be Finalized)

**Heading Font (Modern & Bold)**
- Consider: Poppins, Montserrat, Inter (geometric, modern feel)
- Size: 32px-64px
- Weight: 600-700 (semi-bold to bold)

**Body Font (Readable & Professional)**
- Consider: Open Sans, Roboto, Lato (clean, accessible)
- Size: 14px-16px
- Weight: 400 (regular)
- Line height: 1.6

**Accent/Brand Font (Optional)**
- Consider: Oswald, Bebas Neue (for impact, limited use)
- Use for: Hero text, special highlights only

### Typography Examples
```
HEADING (Large)
Big, Bold, Eye-catching
Your Service Title Here

Body Copy
Clean, readable, easy to scan. This is where you describe your services 
and tell your story. Keep it professional but warm and approachable.

Accent Text
LIMITED USE - Only for special emphasis
```

## Brand Applications

### Logo Placement Rules
- **Minimum Size:** 120px width
- **Clear Space:** Margin around logo = 1/4 logo width
- **No Distortion:** Never stretch, skew, or rotate
- **Color:** Use primary color or black (never thin lines on complex backgrounds)

### Website Guidelines
- [ ] Consistent header/navigation
- [ ] Clear visual hierarchy
- [ ] 1-2 primary CTA buttons (colored with accent)
- [ ] Consistent form styling
- [ ] Footer with all links & social media

### Social Media Branding
- [ ] Profile picture: Logo or icon
- [ ] Header/Banner: Brand colors, 1200x400px recommended
- [ ] Post templates: Consistent layout, color usage
- [ ] Story templates: Consistent fonts, frames
- [ ] Highlight icons: Custom designed

### Business Card (Offline)
- Minimum viable elements: Logo, name, title, email, phone
- Card size: Standard 3.5" x 2"
- Colors: Primary + accent on one side, neutral on back
- Font: Heading font for contact info
