About

Small service businesses don’t want a designer every week; they want templates that stay on-brand when they edit them. Copperleaf is a concept UK grounds-maintenance firm, and this is a year of social ads its owner can run himself. Ainsworth Clinic

[2026] Concept / Booking page for a regulated clinic

Discipline

Web design & development

Status

Concept brand — not a real clinic; the practitioner, address and numbers are illustrative

About

Aesthetics pages usually sell first and explain later. This one is built the way a nurse-led clinic actually works: every enquiry starts with a free clinical consultation, and the page says so before it says anything about price. Treatments carry indicative prices with the terms attached, so a visitor can judge affordability without being quoted a figure the clinic cannot honour.

Built for the rules

Prescription-only treatments are never named with a price, because promoting them to the public is what UK regulators act on. There is no before-and-after gallery: invented patient photos are dishonest and real ones carry a consent burden, so the page explains instead that cases are shown at consultation. Finance options state terms rather than rates.

Bracket

[2026] Concept / Field-service app design

Discipline

Product & interface design

Status

Concept brand — a design study, not a shipped app

About

Scheduling software for HVAC, plumbing and electrical firms is written for the office and then squeezed onto a phone. Bracket is designed the other way round: one job on screen at a time, tap targets sized for gloved hands, and checklists and photos in place of free-text notes, so a technician can finish the paperwork standing in a plant room.

Method

Tokens first, then components, then screens — one accent colour, semantic status colours and a mono face for every figure, so six screens read as one product.

TACET One

[2026] Concept / Cinematic AI product commercial

Discipline

Art direction, AI video production & sound design

Status

Concept brand — the product does not exist and nothing is for sale

About

A 15-second commercial for premium noise-cancelling headphones, made without a product sample, a studio or a crew. The idea is the silence itself: six seconds of muffled city noise, then the sound cuts out, a bass note lands and dust lifts off the ear cushion. The noise-cancelling promise is carried by the sound design rather than a specification, so the film makes no claim it cannot support.

Keeping the product exact

One locked product reference drives every shot. The design was chosen for what the tools hold: matte finishes, because gloss drifts between frames, and no printed text on the product, because the wordmark belongs in post. Camera moves stay slow — pushes and a 20° orbit — and every clip was checked frame by frame for warping.

Deliverables

15s in 9:16 and 1:1, original sound design, voiceover, four hero stills

Made with

ChatGPT Images, Kling image-to-video, ElevenLabs, edit and sound design in post

Kestrel Freight

[2026] Concept / Pricing-led landing page

Discipline

Web design & development

Status

Concept brand — not a trading business, no booking can be made

About

Every UK pallet carrier hides its pricing behind a quote form and a callback, so comparing three of them takes two days. Kestrel puts the number on the page: pick a pallet class, a distance band and a service tier, and the price assembles itself line by line — base, distance, service, access surcharges, fuel — with the transit estimate moving alongside. Every charge the industry applies is disclosed before booking rather than discovered on the invoice.
