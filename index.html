Copperleaf Grounds

[2026] Concept / Editable seasonal template kit

Discipline

Graphic design · social ad creative · template systems

Status

Concept brand — not a trading business, no booking can be made

About

Small service businesses don’t want a designer every week; they want templates that stay on-brand when they edit them. Copperleaf is a concept UK grounds-maintenance firm, and this is a year of social ads its owner can run himself. One master layout — photo, a single headline, one line of detail, one button — rolled into four seasonal campaigns: a spring lawn reset, summer holiday cover, autumn leaf clearance and winter gritting. Each season carries its own photograph and a headline colour that reads as the weather, while the layout, type and button stay identical.

Kit

12 editable templates — four seasons × 4:5 feed ad, square post and 9:16 story, with story text clear of the interface

Built in

Canva, free plan — handed over as editable files plus PNG exports

Photography

Unsplash (Jemima Whyles, Ashkan) and Canva

Kestrel Freight

[2026] Concept / Pricing-led landing page

Discipline

Web design & development

Status

Concept brand — not a trading business, no booking can be made

About

Every UK pallet carrier hides its pricing behind a quote form and a callback, so comparing three of them takes two days. Kestrel puts the number on the page: pick a pallet class, a distance band and a service tier, and the price assembles itself line by line — base, distance, service, access surcharges, fuel — with the transit estimate moving alongside. Every charge the industry applies is disclosed before booking rather than discovered on the invoice.

Page

Live quote calculator, pallet class table, surcharge list, FAQ

Built in

Hand-coded HTML, CSS & JavaScript, deployed on Vercel

Tested

320px to 1920px
