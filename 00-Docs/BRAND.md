## Logo

A gold chevron "V" with a cyan circuit trace and glowing node running through it, over the
wordmark **VIVID**(white)**FLOW**(gold), with **STUDIO** in cyan beneath a gold rule, and the
tagline below in white letterspaced caps.

The artwork sits on pure black. Keep page backgrounds at `#000000` where the logo appears, or
the artwork shows a visible rectangular seam. Never stretch, skew or recolour it. Minimum mark
height 28px.

## Colour

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#000000` | Page background |
| Charcoal | `#0C0E12` | Alternating section bands |
| Surface | `#111419` | Cards, panels |
| Line | `#1C2027` | Borders, dividers |
| **Gold** | `#D4AF37` | Primary accent — buttons, links, headings emphasis |
| Gold light | `#F0D879` | Hover states |
| **Cyan** | `#22D3EE` | Secondary accent — labels, step numbers, tags |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Headings |
| Grey | `#8B9099` | Body text |
| Dim | `#5C626C` | Meta, captions |

Gold leads, cyan supports. Both come straight out of the logo. Do not introduce a third accent,
and do not swap gold or cyan for another hue without redrawing the logo first — a warm/light
palette in particular will not carry this mark.

## Type

- **Outfit** throughout — Google Fonts, weights 200;300;400;500;600;700
- Headings: weight 200–300, uppercase, `letter-spacing: .02em`, tight line-height
- Body: weight 300, `line-height: 1.65`
- Labels: 10px, `letter-spacing: .34em`, uppercase, cyan, preceded by a gold rule
- Buttons: `.72rem`, `letter-spacing: .2em`, uppercase

Sharp corners — 2–3px radii, not pills. The look is precise and engineered, not soft.

## Voice

Professional, friendly, creative, honest. Short lines. Concrete nouns.

**Write:** "Three disciplines. One flow." · "From brief to launch." · "Motion that stops the scroll."

**Never write:** corporate jargon, over-technical language, overpromises ("guaranteed results"),
clichés ("think outside the box"), invented statistics or fake client logos.
