## WEB DESIGN & LANDING PAGES

### Beginner Projects (Week 1-2)
1. **Single-Page Portfolio for Freelancer**
   - Difficulty: Easy
   - Time: 3-4 days
   - Tools: Figma + Webflow
   - Skills: Layout, responsive design, basic interactions
   - Real-world value: Portfolio for photographer, writer, or designer

2. **SaaS Landing Page (Template-based)**
   - Difficulty: Easy
   - Time: 2-3 days
   - Tools: Figma + Webflow
   - Skills: UI hierarchy, CTA design, hero section
   - Real-world value: Tech startup landing pages are in high demand

---

### Intermediate Projects (Week 3-4)
3. **Agency Website (Multi-page)**
   - Difficulty: Medium
   - Time: 1 week
   - Pages: Home, Services, Portfolio, About, Contact
   - Skills: Navigation, service showcase, testimonials, contact forms
   - Real-world value: Small agency template can be resold

4. **E-commerce Product Page**
   - Difficulty: Medium
   - Time: 4-5 days
   - Tools: Webflow + e-commerce setup
   - Skills: Product showcase, pricing, checkout flow
   - Real-world value: High demand from online sellers

---

### Advanced Projects (Week 5+)
5. **Restaurant/Local Business Website**
   - Difficulty: Medium
   - Time: 5-6 days
   - Features: Menu, reservations, Google Maps, photos
   - Skills: Content organization, local SEO basics
   - Real-world value: Local businesses constantly need sites

6. **Course/Membership Site**
   - Difficulty: Hard
   - Time: 1-2 weeks
   - Features: Course modules, member area, payment integration
   - Skills: UX for complex flows, membership logic
   - Real-world value: Creators launching online courses

## VIDEO CONTENT & EDITING

### Beginner Projects (Week 1-2)
1. **Social Media Reel (15-30 seconds)**
   - Difficulty: Easy
   - Time: 1-2 days
   - Tools: CapCut or DaVinci Resolve
   - Skills: Transitions, pacing, music sync, text overlays
   - Real-world value: Influencers & small businesses need reels

2. **YouTube Thumbnail Designs (5-10 variations)**
   - Difficulty: Easy
   - Time: 2-3 days
   - Tools: Figma or Canva
   - Skills: Hierarchy, contrast, typography
   - Real-world value: Every YouTube channel needs thumbnails

---

### Intermediate Projects (Week 3-4)
3. **Product Demo Video (30-60 seconds)**
   - Difficulty: Medium
   - Time: 3-4 days
   - Tools: CapCut or Premiere Pro
   - Skills: Screen recording, voiceover, callouts, B-roll
   - Real-world value: SaaS & app companies need these constantly

4. **Brand/Promotional Video (60 seconds)**
   - Difficulty: Medium
   - Time: 4-5 days
   - Tools: DaVinci Resolve
   - Skills: Color grading, pacing, motion graphics basics
   - Real-world value: Local businesses, YouTube creators, agencies

---

### Advanced Projects (Week 5+)
5. **YouTube Video (5-10 minutes)**
   - Difficulty: Hard
   - Time: 1-2 weeks
   - Tools: DaVinci Resolve with motion graphics
   - Skills: Pacing, storytelling, transitions, music selection
   - Real-world value: YouTube creators & content agencies

6. **Animated Explainer Video (2-3 minutes)**
   - Difficulty: Hard
   - Time: 2-3 weeks
   - Tools: Adobe Animate or CapCut with animation
   - Skills: Animation, voiceover sync, motion graphics
   - Real-world value: High-ticket item, SaaS/education market

## GRAPHIC DESIGN & IMAGE ADS

### Beginner Projects (Week 1-2)
1. **Social Media Graphics Bundle (10-15 designs)**
   - Difficulty: Easy
   - Time: 2-3 days
   - Types: Instagram posts, Stories, Reels covers
   - Tools: Figma or Canva
   - Skills: Template creation, branding consistency
   - Real-world value: Content creators need batches of graphics

2. **Google Ads Banner (3 sizes)**
   - Difficulty: Easy
   - Time: 1-2 days
   - Sizes: 300x250, 728x90, 320x50
   - Tools: Figma or Photoshop
   - Skills: Hierarchy, CTA design, ad psychology
   - Real-world value: E-commerce businesses constantly run ads

---

### Intermediate Projects (Week 3-4)
3. **Email Marketing Graphics (5-7 designs)**
   - Difficulty: Medium
   - Time: 3-4 days
   - Types: Headers, CTAs, social blocks, footer
   - Tools: Figma or Canva
   - Skills: Email optimization, brand consistency
   - Real-world value: Email campaigns need custom graphics

4. **Social Media Ad Campaign (5-10 variations)**
   - Difficulty: Medium
   - Time: 4-5 days
   - Platforms: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn
   - Tools: Figma or Adobe
   - Skills: A/B testing design, platform optimization
   - Real-world value: Digital marketers need variation sets

---

### Advanced Projects (Week 5+)
5. **Brand Identity Package**
   - Difficulty: Hard
   - Time: 1-2 weeks
   - Elements: Logo, color palette, typography, patterns
   - Tools: Figma
   - Skills: Logo design, brand strategy, design systems
   - Real-world value: Premium service, high ticket price

6. **Infographic/Data Visualization**
   - Difficulty: Hard
   - Time: 1 week
   - Tools: Figma or Illustrator
   - Skills: Data interpretation, visual hierarchy, iconography
   - Real-world value: Agencies & brands need infographics

## Where to Get Project Ideas

### Free Sources
- Dribbble, Behance, Pinterest (inspiration)
- Reddit design briefs
- Real local businesses (redesign for portfolio)
- Friends/family (free/discounted first projects)

### Paid Micro-Projects
- 99designs (design contests)
- Fiverr (build portfolio, low pay initially)
- Upwork (small starter projects)
- Freelancer.com

### Self-Directed Projects
- Design your own brand/portfolio
- Redesign a popular website
- Create concepts for fictional companies
- Design for non-profits (free work)

## Portfolio Prioritization

### Must-Have (Before Taking Clients)
- [ ] 2-3 web design pieces
- [ ] 2-3 video editing pieces OR graphics pieces
- [ ] 1 complete brand/multi-project example

### Nice-to-Have (First 3 Months)
- [ ] 3-5 total web projects (variety of styles/industries)
- [ ] 5-7 video/graphics projects (show range)
- [ ] Case studies with process documentation

### Aspirational (6+ Months)
- [ ] 15+ diverse portfolio pieces
- [ ] 10+ testimonials/client reviews
- [ ] 3-5 before/after case studies
